# Sample recording

`sample-session.csv` is a real Mind Monitor export from a Muse S headband, published so the viewer can be
tried without a recording of your own. The "See a sample session" button on the site loads it.

- 69 minutes, 4,143 rows at roughly 1 Hz, 59 columns
- Band powers for all four electrodes, raw EEG, accelerometer and gyroscope, PPG optics, heart rate,
  contact quality and battery
- 541 blink events and 6 jaw-clench events in the `Elements` column

It is shared by the repository owner for demonstration purposes.
